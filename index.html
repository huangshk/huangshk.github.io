About Me

I am Shuokang, an AI/ML researcher based in London. My research focuses on time-series representation learning, with particular interests in generative models, semi-supervised learning, and multimodal modeling. I aim to develop novel and practical methods that connect rigorous research with real-world impact.

I have experience in human behavior analysis using high-dimensional multivariate data, including radio frequency signals and videos. Now, I am doing quantitative research in the finance industry, where I model market microstructure and investigate deep learning to capture alpha signals. I am always happy to discuss research ideas and potential collaborations.
